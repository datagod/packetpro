Search syntax

Searches use SQLite full-text syntax directly. Combine terms with AND, OR, and NOT, or group with parentheses.

Basics

weather stormBoth words must appear (implicit AND).
weather OR stormEither word can appear.
weather NOT testMust include weather, exclude test.
"severe weather"Exact phrase match.
(rain OR snow) AND warningGroup conditions with parentheses.

Wildcards and fields

storm*Prefix match: storm, storms, etc.
original_name:invoiceSearch filenames only (all searches include filenames by default).

Examples

hurricane* AND "wind speed"Prefix plus exact phrase.
original_name:png AND radarPNG filenames containing radar.
(flood OR hurricane) NOT drillEither term, excluding drill.

Malformed queries show an error above the results. Words are stemmed automatically (e.g. running may match run).
